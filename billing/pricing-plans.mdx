# Pricing Plans

BestChatBot offers flexible plans to match your community's size and needs. All plans include core AI chatbot functionality. Higher tiers unlock more capacity and advanced features.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Free | Starter | Pro ⭐ | Business |
|---------|------|---------|--------|----------|
| **Monthly Price** | $0 | $39 | $79 | $179 |
| **Discord Responses/mo** | 20 | 200 | 500 | 1,000 |
| **Web Widget Responses/mo** | 75 | 800 | 2,000 | 5,000 |
| **Scraped Pages** | 5 | 30 | 75 | 150 |
| **Daily Rebuilds** | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| **Knowledge Base Size** | 5 MB | 15 MB | 30 MB | 50 MB |
| **Workspaces** | 1 | 2 | 5 | 10 |

<Note>The **Pro** plan is the most popular choice for growing communities.</Note>
