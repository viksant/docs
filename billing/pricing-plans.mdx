# Pricing Plans

BestChatBot offers flexible plans to match your community's size and needs. All plans include core AI chatbot functionality. Higher tiers unlock more capacity and advanced features.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Free | Starter | Pro ⭐ | Enterprise |
|---------|------|---------|--------|------------|
| **Monthly Price** | $0 | $49 | $99 | Contact Us |
| **Bot Responses** | 10 | 100 | 250 | 500 |
| **Scraped Pages** | 5 | 25 | 50 | 200 |
| **Daily Rebuilds** | 1 | 3 | 10 | 20 |
| **Knowledge Base Size** | 500 KB | 10 MB | 25 MB | 75 MB |
| **Workspaces** | 1 | 2 | 3 | 10 |

<Note>The **Pro** plan is the most popular choice for growing communities.</Note>

## What's Included in Each Plan

### Free
Everything you need to get started with AI-powered support.
- 10 bot responses per month
- 5 scraped pages
- Document upload (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD)
- 1 workspace
- Basic AI chat support

### Starter
For SaaS and technology companies getting started.
- 100 bot responses per month
- 25 scraped pages
- Discord integration
- 2 workspaces
- Basic knowledge graph

### Pro
For professional teams and franchises.
- 250 bot responses per month
- 50 scraped pages
- Advanced web scraping
- 3 workspaces
- Response style and tone customization
- Visual knowledge graphs
- Real-time analytics

### Enterprise
For large-scale deployments with custom needs.
- 500 bot responses per month
- 200 scraped pages
- 20 daily rebuilds
- 10 workspaces
- Priority support and SLA
- Custom API access
- Advanced security and compliance
- Custom terms
