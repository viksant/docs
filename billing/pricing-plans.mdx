# Pricing Plans

BestChatBot offers flexible plans to match your traffic and needs. All plans include core AI chatbot functionality. Higher tiers add more capacity and advanced features.

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Free | Starter | Pro ⭐ | Business |
|---------|------|---------|--------|----------|
| **Monthly Price** | $0 | $39 | $79 | $179 |
| **Widget Responses/mo** | 75 | 800 | 2,000 | 5,000 |
| **Scraped Pages** | 5 | 30 | 75 | 150 |
| **Daily Rebuilds** | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| **Knowledge Base Size** | 1 MB | 15 MB | 30 MB | 50 MB |
| **Workspaces** | 1 | 2 | 5 | 10 |
| **Invitable Members** | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| **Agentic Integrations** | — | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Hide "Powered by" badge** | — | — | ✅ | ✅ |

<Note>The **Pro** plan is the most popular choice for growing sites.</Note>

<Info>**Invitable members** are in addition to the workspace owner, who never counts toward the limit. A Pro workspace can have you plus 2 teammates. **Agentic Integrations** let your bot take real actions (book meetings, open tickets, look up orders) and are included in **Pro** and **Business**. See [Members & Roles](/workspace/members-roles) and [Agentic Actions](/integrations/agentic-actions).</Info>

## Choosing the Right Plan

  </Card>
