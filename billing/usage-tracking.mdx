# Usage Tracking

BestChatBot tracks your **monthly bot responses** so you always know how much of your plan you've used. Usage resets at the beginning of each billing cycle.

## Viewing Your Usage

In the dashboard, you'll find usage information in several places:

### Usage Indicator
A quick-glance indicator in the sidebar or top bar shows your current usage as a percentage of your plan limit.

### Usage Chart
The **Billing** section includes a chart showing your response usage over time, so you can see trends and predict when you might approach your limit.

### Workspace Distribution
If you have multiple workspaces, you can see how usage is distributed across them. This helps you identify which communities are most active.

## What Counts as a Response

Each time the bot sends a reply — whether in Discord or via the web widget — it counts as **one bot response**. The following do **not** count:

| Action | Counts as Response? |
|--------|---------------------|
| Bot replies to a question in Discord | Yes |
| Bot replies via the web widget | Yes |
| Testing in the Retrieval Testing page | No |
| Bot skips a message (no relevant answer) | No |
| Rebuilding the knowledge base | No |
| Uploading documents | No |

<Tip>Use the **Retrieval Testing** page to test as much as you want. It doesn't affect your response count.</Tip>

## Approaching Your Limit

When you're nearing your monthly limit:

- The usage indicator changes color to warn you
- You can upgrade your plan at any time to get more responses

<Warning>Once you hit your monthly limit, the bot will stop responding in Discord until the next billing cycle or until you upgrade.</Warning>
