# 5-Minute Setup Guide

Here is the fastest way to go from zero to a working AI chatbot.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account" icon="user-plus">
    Go to [bestchatbot.io](https://bestchatbot.io) and click **Get Started**. Sign up with your **Google account** or **email and password**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a Workspace" icon="grid-2">
    After signing in, create a new workspace. A workspace holds your knowledge base, settings, and integrations.

    <Tip>You can deploy both a Discord bot and a web widget from the same workspace. One knowledge base, multiple channels.</Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Your Platform" icon="window-maximize">
    Pick how you want to deploy your chatbot:

    - **Discord** — add a bot to your Discord server
    - **Web Widget** — embed a chat widget on your website

    For Discord, you'll be asked to sign in with your Discord account to authorize the bot on your server. For the web widget, you'll get a code snippet to paste on your site.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Knowledge" icon="book-open">
    Give the bot something to learn from. You have three options:

    - **Upload Documents**: Drag and drop PDFs, DOCX, TXT, or MD files
    - **Add URLs**: Paste a website URL and let BestChatBot scrape the content
    - **Let it learn from Discord**: The bot automatically picks up moderator answers

    <Note>After adding content, click **Rebuild Knowledge Base** so the bot can process your data.</Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test and Go Live" icon="circle-check">
    Use the **Retrieval Testing** page in your dashboard to ask questions and check the bot's answers before going live.

    Once you're satisfied, the bot is already active. No extra step needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
