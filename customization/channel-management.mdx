# Channel Management

Control where BestChatBot responds. The setup depends on your platform.

---

## Discord Channels

The **Platform Channels** page gives you control over which Discord channels BestChatBot monitors and responds in. The bot shows all text channels where it has the **View Channel** permission.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/channel-access-discord.png" alt="Platform Channels page showing Discord text channels with Active and Mod QA Learning toggles" />
</Frame>

### Managing Channels

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Platform Channels">
    Navigate to **Platform Channels** from the dashboard sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Toggle Individual Channels">
    Each channel card has two controls:

    - **Active** — enables or disables bot responses in that channel
    - **Mod QA Learning** — enables or disables automatic learning from moderator answers
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use Bulk Actions">
    For quick changes:
    - **Select All**: Enable the bot in every channel
    - **Deselect All**: Disable the bot everywhere
  </Step>

  <Step title="Changes Apply Immediately">
    Channel changes take effect right away. No rebuild is needed.

    <Tip>Unlike knowledge base changes, channel toggling is instant.</Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Channel Types

| Channel Type | Supported |
|--------------|-----------|
| Text channels | Yes |
| Voice channels | No |
| Announcement channels | No |
| Forum channels | No |
| Stage channels | No |

---

## Web Widget

The web widget does not have per-channel control. Each workspace supports **one widget**, and it is either **active** or **inactive** globally.

To toggle your widget:

1. Go to the **Web Widgets** page from the dashboard sidebar
2. Use the **Active** toggle on your widget card to enable or disable it

When inactive, the widget will not appear on your website.
