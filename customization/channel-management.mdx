# Channel Management

The **Channel Manager** gives you precise control over which Discord channels BestChatBot monitors and responds in.

## The Channel Grid

When you open the Channel Manager, you'll see a grid of all text channels in your Discord server. Each channel appears as a card showing:

- The channel name (matching your Discord server's channel names)
- Whether the channel is **enabled** (active) or **disabled** (inactive)

## Managing Channels

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channel Manager">
    Navigate to **Channel Management** from the dashboard sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Toggle Individual Channels">
    Click on any channel card to flip it between enabled and disabled. Enabled channels are visually highlighted.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use Bulk Actions">
    For quick changes:
    - **Select All**:Enable the bot in every channel
    - **Deselect All**:Disable the bot everywhere
  </Step>

  <Step title="Changes Apply Immediately">
    Channel changes take effect right away. No rebuild is needed.

    <Tip>Unlike knowledge base changes, channel toggling is instant. The bot starts or stops responding in channels as soon as you toggle them.</Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Channel Strategy

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Start Small" icon="seedling">
    Begin with 1-2 channels to test the bot's responses. Expand gradually as you're satisfied with quality.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Separate Support Channels" icon="headset">
    Enable the bot in dedicated support or FAQ channels where questions are expected.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Avoid Chat Channels" icon="comment-slash">
    General chat channels with casual conversation may not benefit from bot responses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review Regularly" icon="calendar-check">
    As your server grows and adds new channels, revisit the Channel Manager to include them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Channel Types

The Channel Manager only shows **text channels**. Other channel types are not supported:

| Channel Type | Supported |
|--------------|-----------|
| Text channels | Yes |
| Voice channels | No |
| Announcement channels | No |
| Forum channels | No |
| Stage channels | No |
