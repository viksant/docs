# Response Preferences

The **Preferences** panel lets you control how your bot communicates with your community. You can adjust the communication style and response length to match your brand's voice.

## Communication Style

Choose how the bot talks to your users:

| Style | Description | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Professional** | Formal, polished language | Business communities, SaaS support |
| **Casual** | Friendly, conversational tone | Gaming communities, social servers |
| **Technical** | Precise, detail-oriented language | Developer communities, technical products |
| **Concise** | Short, to-the-point answers | High-volume servers, quick FAQ bots |

## Response Length

Control how detailed the bot's answers are:

| Length | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **Brief** | Short, direct answers in 1-2 sentences | Simple FAQs, quick support |
| **Moderate** | Balanced answers with enough context | Most use cases (default) |
| **Detailed** | Full answers with thorough explanations | Complex products, educational content |

## How to Change Preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Preferences">
    Click the **Preferences** icon (gear icon) in the dashboard. A side panel will open.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Communication Style">
    Use the **Communication Style** dropdown to choose between Professional, Casual, Technical, or Concise.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Response Length">
    Use the **Response Length** dropdown to choose between Brief, Moderate, or Detailed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save Changes">
    Click the **Save** button. Changes take effect immediately for all new responses.

    <Note>Existing responses in Discord won't change. Only new answers will use the updated style.</Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Per-Workspace Settings

Preferences are configured **per workspace**. If you have multiple Discord servers connected to BestChatBot, each one can have its own communication style and response length.

<Tip>A gaming community might use **Casual** + **Brief**, while a SaaS product's support server might use **Professional** + **Detailed**.</Tip>
