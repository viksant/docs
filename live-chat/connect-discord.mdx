# Connect Discord

Human handoff can live where your team already hangs out. Connect Discord and operators can pick up live conversations **from a Discord server** — replies they post in the thread reach the visitor in the widget, and the conversation still shows up in your dashboard inbox.

<Note>Discord live-chat handoff is a **premium** feature, available on the **Pro** and **Business** plans. See [Pricing Plans](/billing/pricing-plans).</Note>

<Note>Discord and Slack handoff are **mutually exclusive**. Connecting Discord turns off Slack handoff for this workspace (and vice versa) — live chat routes to one place at a time.</Note>

## How It Works

When a visitor asks for a human, BestChatBot opens **one thread per conversation** in your chosen channel. An operator replies in that thread; the visitor sees the replies in the widget, and messages stay in sync with your dashboard inbox.

Connecting takes two phases: first you **install the bot** in your server, then you **choose a channel and an operator role**. Handoffs stay off until both are done.

## Connect Discord to Your Widget

<Note>You need an **Editor**, **Admin**, or **Owner** role, on a plan that includes Discord. Adding a bot to a server also requires the **Manage Server** permission in Discord itself.</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Discord tab" icon="discord">
    In your workspace dashboard, open **Web Widget → Discord**, then click **Connect Discord**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/discord-tab-not-connected.png" alt="Discord tab in the widget settings with the Connect Discord button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the bot in your server" icon="robot">
    A Discord window opens. Pick **one of your servers** and approve the bot. BestChatBot requests only the permissions it needs to run handoff threads: **View Channel**, **Send Messages**, **Read Message History**, **Create Public/Private Threads**, and **Send Messages in Threads**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/discord-authorize.png" alt="Discord authorization screen approving the BestChatBot bot for a server" />
    </Frame>

    When you return, the Discord tab shows **Setup incomplete** — the bot is in your server, but handoffs stay off until you finish the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a channel and an operator role" icon="hashtag">
    Click **Finish setup** and pick two things, both required:

    - **Channel** — the text channel where handoff threads open. Only text channels appear in the list.
    - **Operator role** — the role whose members are allowed to answer. **Replies from members without this role never reach the visitor.**

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/discord-setup-dialog.png" alt="Dialog to choose the Discord channel and operator role for handoffs" />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>If the bot can't see or post in the channel you picked, setup fails with: *"The bot doesn't have permission for that action in your Discord server. Give the bot's role the 'View Channel' and 'Send Messages' permissions (or the Administrator role) and try again."* Fix the channel's permissions in Discord, then retry.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're connected" icon="circle-check">
    The Discord tab shows **Active**, with your server, channel, and operator role, plus options to **Change channel & role** or **Disconnect**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/discord-tab-connected.png" alt="Discord tab showing the active connection with server, channel and operator role" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Handling a Handoff in Discord

When a visitor requests a human, the bot opens a thread named after the conversation (for example, **Handoff a1b2c3d4**). The first post is a card — *"New handoff - a visitor requested a human. Reply in this thread."* — with the visitor's **Visitor**, **Email**, **ID**, and **First seen** details.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/discord-handoff-thread.png" alt="Discord handoff thread mirroring the live chat — visitor and bot messages carry per-role badges — next to the visitor's widget" />
</Frame>

- **Reply in the thread** to answer the visitor — your message appears in their widget. Only members with the operator role can answer.
- The visitor's messages and any dashboard-operator replies are mirrored into the thread. Because a Discord bot posts under a single identity, each mirrored message carries a small badge showing who sent it (visitor, bot, or operator).
- Click **Resolve** when you're done. The handoff ends and the visitor returns to the bot.

<Note>Discord is an **alternative** to the dashboard, not a replacement. The same conversation still appears in your dashboard's [live inbox](/live-chat/human-handoff), tagged **In Discord** so you can see where it's being handled.</Note>

## Privacy

Handoff threads are **public** to your server by design, and the visitor's email and ID appear in the thread's first card. Use a **dedicated channel that only your operator role can see**, so visitor details aren't exposed to your whole server. When you enable Discord handoff, visitor conversation data is shared with Discord to deliver it to your channel; Discord is listed as a third-party service in our Privacy Policy.

## Disconnecting

Open **Web Widget → Discord** and click **Disconnect**. Handoffs stop going to Discord right away and return to dashboard-only. You can reconnect anytime, but you'll choose the channel and operator role again.
