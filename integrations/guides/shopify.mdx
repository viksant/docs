# Shopify

**Category: E-commerce.** Connect Shopify and your widget can browse products and track orders for visitors, right inside the chat.

<Note>Integrations are available on the **Pro** and **Business** plans. Shopify shares the E-commerce category with WooCommerce, so only one of the two can be active at a time.</Note>

## What Your Bot Can Do

| Action | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **Browse products** | Help the visitor find products from your catalog. |
| **Check order status** | Look up an order's status by the visitor's email. |

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations" icon="plug">
    In your workspace dashboard, open the **Integrations** tab and find **Shopify**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect" icon="link">
    Click **Connect**. A secure popup opens. Sign in to Shopify and authorize access. You never type credentials into BestChatBot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're live" icon="circle-check">
    Once connected, Shopify's actions become available to your widget right away. If WooCommerce was connected, it's disconnected automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Need a different role to connect? Connecting integrations requires an **Editor**, **Admin**, or **Owner** role. See [Members & Roles](/workspace/members-roles).</Note>

## One per Category

Shopify is in the **E-commerce** category together with WooCommerce. Connecting one disconnects the other, so your bot always has a single, clear tool for e-commerce.

<Warning>If you connect Shopify while WooCommerce is active, WooCommerce is turned off automatically.</Warning>
