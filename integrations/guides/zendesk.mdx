# Zendesk

**Category: Help Desk.** Connect Zendesk and your widget can open and track support tickets for visitors, right inside the chat.

<Note>Integrations are available on the **Pro** and **Business** plans. Zendesk shares the Help Desk category with Freshdesk, so only one of the two can be active at a time.</Note>

## What Your Bot Can Do

| Action | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **Open a support ticket** | Turn a visitor's issue into a tracked ticket. |
| **Check a ticket's status** | Look up the status of an existing ticket. |

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations" icon="plug">
    In your workspace dashboard, open the **Integrations** tab and find **Zendesk**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect" icon="link">
    Click **Connect**. A secure popup opens. Sign in to Zendesk and authorize access. You never type credentials into BestChatBot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're live" icon="circle-check">
    Once connected, Zendesk's actions become available to your widget right away. If Freshdesk was connected, it's disconnected automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Need a different role to connect? Connecting integrations requires an **Editor**, **Admin**, or **Owner** role. See [Members & Roles](/workspace/members-roles).</Note>

## One per Category

Zendesk is in the **Help Desk** category together with Freshdesk. Connecting one disconnects the other, so your bot always has a single, clear tool for help desk.

<Warning>If you connect Zendesk while Freshdesk is active, Freshdesk is turned off automatically.</Warning>
