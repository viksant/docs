# Stripe

**Category: Payments.** Connect Stripe and your widget can handle billing and subscriptions for visitors, right inside the chat.

<Note>Integrations are available on the **Pro** and **Business** plans.</Note>

## What Your Bot Can Do

| Action | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **View a subscription** | Show the visitor their current plan. |
| **View invoices and payment methods** | Let the visitor see invoices and saved payment methods. |
| **Manage subscription** | Open the billing portal so the visitor can update their plan. |
| **Start a purchase** | Generate a checkout link for the visitor. |
| **Browse products and prices** | List what's available to buy. |

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations" icon="plug">
    In your workspace dashboard, open the **Integrations** tab and find **Stripe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect" icon="link">
    Click **Connect**. A secure popup opens. Enter your Stripe API key. Your credentials go to the secure form, never into the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're live" icon="circle-check">
    Once connected, Stripe's actions become available to your widget right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Need a different role to connect? Connecting integrations requires an **Editor**, **Admin**, or **Owner** role. See [Members & Roles](/workspace/members-roles).</Note>

## One per Category

Stripe is the only option in the **Payments** category, so it never conflicts with another integration.
