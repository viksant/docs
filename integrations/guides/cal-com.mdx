# Cal.com

**Category: Scheduling.** Connect Cal.com and your widget can book and manage meetings for visitors, right inside the chat.

<Note>Integrations are available on the **Pro** and **Business** plans. Cal.com shares the Scheduling category with Calendly, so only one of the two can be active at a time.</Note>

## What Your Bot Can Do

| Action | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **Check availability** | Show the visitor open time slots from your calendar. |
| **Book a meeting** | Schedule the meeting and confirm it in the chat. |
| **Cancel a meeting** | Let the visitor cancel a meeting they booked. |

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations" icon="plug">
    In your workspace dashboard, open the **Integrations** tab and find **Cal.com**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect" icon="link">
    Click **Connect**. A secure popup opens. Enter your Cal.com API key. Your credentials go to the secure form, never into the chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're live" icon="circle-check">
    Once connected, Cal.com's actions become available to your widget right away. If Calendly was connected, it's disconnected automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Need a different role to connect? Connecting integrations requires an **Editor**, **Admin**, or **Owner** role. See [Members & Roles](/workspace/members-roles).</Note>

## One per Category

Cal.com is in the **Scheduling** category together with Calendly. Connecting one disconnects the other, so your bot always has a single, clear tool for scheduling.

<Warning>If you connect Cal.com while Calendly is active, Calendly is turned off automatically.</Warning>
