# HubSpot

**Category: CRM.** Connect HubSpot and your widget can capture leads for visitors, right inside the chat.

<Note>Integrations are available on the **Pro** and **Business** plans.</Note>

## What This Bot Can Do

The bot does exactly these actions, nothing more. "Needs sign-in" means the visitor must be identified first; otherwise the bot gives a safe, neutral answer.

| Action | What it does | Needs sign-in |
|--------|--------------|:-------------:|
| **Capture a lead** | Save the visitor as a new contact with their name, email, company, and what they're interested in. | No |

## What It Can't Do

HubSpot is scoped to the actions above. It does **not**:

- Look up, search, or update existing contacts
- View deals, companies, or pipelines
- Do anything beyond capturing a new lead

## How to Connect

<Note>Connecting an integration requires an **Editor**, **Admin**, or **Owner** role, on the **Pro** or **Business** plan. See [Members & Roles](/workspace/members-roles).</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations" icon="plug">
    In your workspace dashboard, open your **Web Widget → Integrations** tab and find **HubSpot**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and authorize" icon="link">
    Click **Connect**. A secure popup opens — sign in to HubSpot, choose the account, and authorize access. You never type credentials into BestChatBot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once connected, HubSpot's lead capture becomes available to your widget right away.
