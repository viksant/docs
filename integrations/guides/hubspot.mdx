# HubSpot

**Category: CRM.** Connect HubSpot and your widget can capture leads for visitors, right inside the chat.

<Note>Integrations are available on the **Pro** and **Business** plans.</Note>

## What Your Bot Can Do

| Action | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **Capture a lead** | Save the visitor's details so your sales team can follow up. |

## How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations" icon="plug">
    In your workspace dashboard, open the **Integrations** tab and find **HubSpot**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect" icon="link">
    Click **Connect**. A secure popup opens. Sign in to HubSpot and authorize access. You never type credentials into BestChatBot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're live" icon="circle-check">
    Once connected, HubSpot's actions become available to your widget right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Need a different role to connect? Connecting integrations requires an **Editor**, **Admin**, or **Owner** role. See [Members & Roles](/workspace/members-roles).</Note>

## One per Category

HubSpot is the only option in the **CRM** category, so it never conflicts with another integration.
