# Discord

BestChatBot lives inside your Discord server. It reads messages, finds answers from your knowledge base, and replies naturally. No slash commands. No weird syntax. Just plain conversation.

## What the Bot Needs

When you invite BestChatBot, it asks for a specific set of permissions. Here is what each one does:

| Permission | What It Does |
|------------|--------------|
| Read Messages | Lets the bot see questions in your channels |
| Send Messages | Lets the bot reply with answers |
| Read Message History | Gives the bot context from recent messages |
| Embed Links | Lets the bot format responses with rich content |

<Warning>Removing any of these permissions will break the bot. Keep all of them active.</Warning>

## How the Bot Sees Your Server

BestChatBot connects to your server through Discord's bot system. Here is what that means for you:

- The bot appears in your member list like any other bot
- It only reads channels you've enabled in the Channel Manager
- It never reads DMs or channels outside your selected list
- It responds to regular messages, not slash commands

## Bot Status

The bot runs 24/7 on our servers. You don't need to keep your computer on or any app running. Once invited, it stays active as long as your subscription is valid.
