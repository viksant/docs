# Create Your Account

BestChatBot uses **Discord OAuth** for authentication. You sign in with your existing Discord account. No new passwords to remember.

## How to Sign Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit BestChatBot">
    Go to [bestchatbot.io](https://bestchatbot.io) and click the **Get Started** button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign In with Discord">
    You'll be redirected to Discord's authorization page. Click **Authorize** to allow BestChatBot to access your basic profile information.

    <Note>BestChatBot only requests access to your username, avatar, and the servers you manage. It never reads your private messages.</Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Land on Your Dashboard">
    After authorization, you'll be taken directly to the BestChatBot dashboard. Your account is ready to use.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/workspace-selector-screen.png" alt="Workspace selector showing your connected servers" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens During Sign-Up

When you sign in with Discord, BestChatBot automatically:

- Creates your account using your Discord identity
- Detects the servers where you have **admin or management permissions**
- Sets up your first workspace

<Tip>A **workspace** in BestChatBot maps to a Discord server. Each server you connect becomes its own workspace with separate knowledge base, settings, and billing.</Tip>
