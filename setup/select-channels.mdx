# Select Active Channels

After inviting the bot, you need to tell it which channels to monitor. BestChatBot will only respond to messages in channels you explicitly enable.

## Using the Channel Manager

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channel Management">
    In your dashboard, navigate to the **Channel Management** section from the sidebar.
  </Step>

  <Step title="View Your Channels">
    You'll see a grid of all text channels in your Discord server. Each channel is displayed as a card showing the channel name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Toggle Channels On or Off">
    Click on a channel card to **enable** or **disable** it. Enabled channels are highlighted, and the bot will respond to messages in those channels.

    <Tip>Start with one or two low-traffic channels to test the bot before enabling it everywhere.</Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use Bulk Selection (Optional)">
    If you want the bot active in many channels, use the **Select All** option to enable everything at once. You can then deselect specific channels you want to exclude.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the Bot Behaves in Channels

Once a channel is active:

- The bot reads every new message in that channel
- If the message looks like a question the bot can answer, it responds automatically
- The bot **does not** use slash commands. It responds to regular messages naturally

<Note>The bot is helpful without being intrusive. It won't respond to every message. It only jumps in when it has relevant knowledge to share.</Note>

## Channel Categories Not Shown

The channel manager only shows **text channels**. Voice channels, announcement channels, and forum channels are not listed because the bot can only respond in standard text channels.
