## How It Works

Let's break it down into four steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plug In Your Knowledge">
    Upload documents, scrape your website, or let the bot learn from real conversations in your Discord server. The AI agent picks up your business context right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy Anywhere">
    Add the bot to your Discord server, embed a chat widget on your website, or both. One knowledge base powers all channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn in Real Time">
    Every conversation improves future responses. BestChatBot adapts to your community's language, new products, and emerging questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scale Without Limits">
    Handle 10 conversations or 10,000 at the same time. No hiring, no training.
  </Step>
</Steps>
