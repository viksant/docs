# Welcome to BestChatBot

BestChatBot is an **AI support agent** that learns from your business knowledge and gives instant, human-like answers to your website visitors around the clock.

It's not just another chatbot. It's a **learning AI agent** trained on your own data. It gets better with every real conversation.

## How It Works

Let's break it down into four steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plug In Your Knowledge">
    Upload documents or scrape your website URLs. The AI agent picks up your business context right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy on Your Site">
    Embed a chat widget on your website by pasting a single script tag. Each workspace has its own isolated knowledge base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the Experience">
    Match your brand color, set the tone (Professional, Casual, Technical, Concise) and response length. Tweak anytime.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scale Without Limits">
    Handle 10 conversations or 10,000 at the same time. No hiring, no training.
  </Step>
</Steps>
