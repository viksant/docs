# Bot Responses

The **Bot Responses** page shows you every answer your bot has given in Discord, along with performance analytics to help you understand how it's performing.

## Response List

The main view shows a chronological list of all bot responses. For each response, you can see:

- **The question**:What the user asked
- **The answer**:What the bot replied
- **The channel**:Where the conversation happened
- **Timestamp**:When the response was sent
- **Sources cited**:What knowledge the bot drew from

## Analytics Overview

At the top of the page, a **stats panel** gives you a quick overview:

| Metric | What It Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| Total Responses | How many answers the bot has given this month |
| Response Rate | How often the bot successfully answers questions |
| Average Response Time | How quickly the bot responds |

## Why This Matters

Monitoring bot responses helps you:

- **Identify knowledge gaps**: Questions the bot can't answer well point to missing content
- **Spot incorrect answers**: Catch bad responses and fix them by updating your knowledge base
- **Track usage**: See how much your community relies on the bot
- **Measure improvement**: Watch response quality go up as you add more content

<Tip>If you see the bot giving incorrect or incomplete answers to a specific topic, add more relevant content to your knowledge base and rebuild.</Tip>
